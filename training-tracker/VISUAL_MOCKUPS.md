## 🎨 Color & Animation Specifications

### Color Palette
- **Primary Navy**: `#1a2332` (Backgrounds, headers)
- **Electric Blue**: `#00d4ff` (Primary actions, links)
- **Emerald Green**: `#00c896` (Success states, completed items)
- **Warm Orange**: `#ff6b35` (Warnings, attention items)
- **Soft Gray**: `#f8f9fb` (Secondary backgrounds)
- **Dark Gray**: `#2d3748` (Text, borders)

### Animation Timings
- **Card Hover**: `transform: translateY(-4px)` in `200ms ease`
- **Progress Rings**: `stroke-dasharray` animation over `1000ms ease-out`
- **Achievement Unlock**: Scale + bounce effect `500ms cubic-bezier(0.68, -0.55, 0.265, 1.55)`
- **Page Transitions**: Slide animations `300ms ease-in-out`

### Typography Scale
- **Display**: 48px/56px (Main headings)
- **Heading 1**: 36px/44px (Section titles)
- **Heading 2**: 24px/32px (Card titles)
- **Body**: 16px/24px (Regular text)
- **Caption**: 14px/20px (Meta information)
